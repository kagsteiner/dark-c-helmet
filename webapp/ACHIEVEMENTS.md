# Achievements

Challenges (games with odds against the real engine) only. Every achievement is earned in one game, and
can be computed from what a game already records (also for games played before).

## Three tabs

Standard · Chess960 · 960² — like the new-game screen. Same achievements in each tab.

## Two dimensions per achievement

- **Takebacks** — three tiers:
  - *Coffeehouse*: any number of takebacks
  - *J'adoube*: at most one takeback
  - *Touch-move*: no takeback
- **Engine time** — each tier shows the longest engine time it was earned at:
  "Touch-move: 1 s ✓ — not yet at 2 s". Engine times: those of the odds sheet (1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30 s, 1, 2, 5, 15 min).

The player's clock does not matter (no clock is fine).

## "Within a pawn"

The engine's evaluation for the player is above −1.00 (a pawn's worth).
